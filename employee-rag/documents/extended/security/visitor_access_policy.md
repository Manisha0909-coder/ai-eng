# Visitor Access Policy

**Version:** 1.1
**Effective Date:** 2025-08-01

## Pre-Registration

All visitors must be pre-registered by their host employee at least **4
hours** before arrival, through the Visitor Management System. Walk-in
visitors without pre-registration must wait at reception while the host is
contacted.

## Sign-In and Badge

Visitors sign in at reception, present a government-issued ID, and receive
a temporary visitor badge. Visitor badges are visually distinct from
employee badges and must be worn at all times on premises.

## Escort Requirement

Visitors must be escorted by their host at all times outside reception and
designated meeting areas. An unescorted visitor seen in a restricted area
should be reported to Security immediately.

## Confidentiality

Visitors accessing any area where Confidential or Restricted information
(per the Data Privacy Policy's classification) might be visible must sign
a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before entry, in addition to the standard
sign-in.

## Checkout

Visitors must sign out and return their visitor badge before leaving.
Reception reports any visitor badge not returned by end of day to
Security, who deactivates it immediately.
