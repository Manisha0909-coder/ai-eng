# Physical Safety Policy

**Version:** 1.5
**Effective Date:** 2025-10-01

## Badge Access

All employees receive a badge for building access on Day 1 (see Onboarding
Policy). Badges must be worn visibly at all times inside company premises.
A lost badge must be reported to Security immediately; a replacement is
issued within **1 business day**, and the lost badge is deactivated
immediately on report.

## Fire Drills

Fire drills are conducted **twice a year** (not to be confused with the
performance review cycle, which also happens twice a year for an unrelated
reason). Attendance is mandatory; floor wardens take attendance during
each drill.

## Emergency Exits and Assembly Points

Emergency exit maps are posted on every floor. In an emergency, proceed to
your floor's designated assembly point and wait for the floor warden's
headcount before re-entering the building.

## Workplace Injury Reporting

Any workplace injury, no matter how minor, must be reported to Security
and HR within **24 hours** — the same reporting window as a lost laptop
under the IT Security Policy, though this is a separate, unrelated
requirement about physical injuries rather than equipment.

## After-Hours Access

Access to the building outside standard hours (8 AM–8 PM) requires
after-hours access approval from your manager, requested through the
Access Management Policy's request process.
