# Remote Work Equipment Policy (SUPERSEDED)

**Version:** 1.0 — **SUPERSEDED as of 2026-03-01**
**Effective Date:** 2023-06-01 to 2026-02-28
**Superseded By:** Remote Work Equipment Policy v2.0 (effective 2026-03-01)

> **This version is no longer in effect.** It is retained for historical
> reference only. See the current Remote Work Equipment Policy for
> applicable amounts.

## Scope

This policy covered equipment and connectivity for employees working from
home, per the frequency allowed in the Employee Leave Policy.

## Equipment Stipend (historical)

Employees working from home received a one-time home-office setup stipend
of **₹8,000** (or **$120** for US-based employees), covering a monitor,
chair, or similar equipment.

## Monthly Connectivity Allowance (historical)

A monthly internet/connectivity allowance of **₹1,000** (or **$15** for
US-based employees) was paid alongside salary.

## VPN and Security Requirements

All remote connections to internal systems were required to use the
company VPN, per the IT Security Policy.
