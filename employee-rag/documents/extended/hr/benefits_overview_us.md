# Benefits Overview (US)

**Version:** 1.3
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

## Applicability

This policy applies to employees whose primary work location is in the
United States. India-based employees should refer to the Benefits Overview
(India) instead — amounts and programs below are not available to
India-based employees.

## Health Insurance

The company offers a choice of two health insurance plans (PPO and HDHP),
with the company covering **80%** of the employee-only premium and **60%**
of dependent premiums. Open enrollment runs every November for coverage
starting the following January.

## 401(k) Retirement Plan

The company matches **50%** of employee 401(k) contributions, up to **6%**
of base salary. Matching contributions vest over **3 years**, at one-third
per year of service.

## Paid Holidays

US-based employees receive **11** paid company holidays per year, in
addition to the annual leave covered in the Employee Leave Policy, which
applies company-wide.

## Commuter Benefits

A pre-tax commuter benefits program is available, covering up to the IRS
monthly pre-tax limit for transit and parking combined.

## Wellness Stipend

A wellness stipend of **$50/month** is available for gym memberships or
fitness-related expenses, submitted through the standard expense process
but tracked separately from the general expense policy's categories.
