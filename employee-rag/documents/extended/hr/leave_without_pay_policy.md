# Leave Without Pay (LWP) Policy

**Version:** 1.2
**Effective Date:** 2025-04-01

## Purpose

Leave without pay (LWP) covers extended time off beyond what annual, sick,
or maternity leave provides, for employees who have exhausted their paid
leave balance or need leave for a reason paid leave doesn't cover (e.g.
extended personal travel, caregiving beyond maternity leave's 12 weeks).

## Eligibility

Employees must have completed their 6-month probation period (see
Onboarding Policy) and exhausted their current annual leave balance before
requesting LWP. LWP is not available during an active Performance
Improvement Plan.

## Requesting LWP

Submit a request to your manager at least **4 weeks** in advance, except
for emergencies, which require only reasonable notice. Requests longer than
**90 days** require department head approval in addition to the manager's.

## Effect on Benefits and Seniority

- Health insurance coverage continues for the first **30 days** of LWP, at
  company cost; beyond 30 days, the employee may continue coverage at their
  own cost or pause it.
- LWP does not count toward tenure for gratuity (India) or 401(k) vesting
  (US) purposes.
- Annual leave does not accrue during LWP.

## Maximum Duration

LWP cannot exceed **180 days** in a rolling 12-month period. An employee
who needs longer than 180 days should discuss alternatives (resignation
with rehire eligibility, or a formal leave of absence approved directly by
HR leadership) with HR.

## Return From LWP

Employees must confirm their return date at least **2 weeks** before the
agreed end date. Failure to return on the agreed date without an approved
extension is treated as voluntary resignation.
