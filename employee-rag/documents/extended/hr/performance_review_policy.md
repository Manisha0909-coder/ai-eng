# Performance Review Policy

**Version:** 1.4
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

## Review Cycle

Performance reviews happen **twice a year**: a mid-year check-in (June) and
an annual review (December). The annual review determines compensation
changes and bonus eligibility; the mid-year check-in is developmental only
and does not affect pay.

## Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | Significantly exceeds expectations |
| 4 | Exceeds | Consistently exceeds expectations |
| 3 | Meets | Meets expectations |
| 2 | Needs Improvement | Below expectations in some areas |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | Consistently below expectations |

## Who Reviews You

Your direct manager completes your review, informed by peer feedback
(minimum 2 peers, maximum 5) collected through the HR portal. Manager
reviews for people managers also include upward feedback from their
direct reports.

## Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

An employee rated **1 (Unsatisfactory)**, or rated **2 (Needs Improvement)**
in two consecutive review cycles, is placed on a Performance Improvement
Plan (PIP). A PIP runs for **60 days**, with a written plan agreed between
the employee, manager, and HR, and a formal check-in at the 30-day mark.
Failure to meet the PIP's goals by day 60 may result in termination, unless
the department head approves an extension in writing.

## Disputing a Rating

An employee who disagrees with their rating may request a review by HR
within **14 days** of receiving it. HR's review is final; there is no
further escalation path for rating disputes (contrast this with the
Grievance Policy's multi-level escalation, which does not apply to
performance ratings specifically).

## Record Retention

Performance review records are retained for **3 years** after the
employee's last review, per the Data Privacy Policy's retention schedule
for HR records.
