# Grievance Policy

**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** 2025-07-01

## Purpose

This policy describes how employees can raise a formal grievance about any
workplace issue — compensation disputes, interpersonal conflicts, unfair
treatment, or dissatisfaction with a manager's decision — separate from the
Code of Conduct's process for reporting harassment or misconduct.

## What Counts as a Grievance

A grievance is a formal, written complaint about a specific decision,
action, or condition affecting the employee's employment. It is not the
right channel for harassment, discrimination, or misconduct reports — those
go through the Code of Conduct's reporting process (HR or the anonymous
ethics hotline) instead.

## Filing a Grievance

1. Submit a written grievance to your manager within **30 days** of the
   incident, using the Grievance Form available on the HR portal.
2. If the grievance involves your manager directly, submit it to HR
   instead.
3. HR acknowledges receipt within **3 business days**.

## Escalation Levels

| Level | Reviewer | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Direct manager | 7 business days |
| 2 | Department head | 10 business days |
| 3 | HR Grievance Committee | 15 business days |

Unless HR grants an extension in writing, each level must respond within
its stated window or the grievance automatically escalates to the next
level.

## India-Specific Note: POSH Committee

For grievances specifically related to sexual harassment, India-based
employees may file directly with the Internal Committee constituted under
the POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) Act, bypassing the standard
escalation levels above. Contact HR for the current committee roster.

## Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who files a grievance in good faith is strictly
prohibited, consistent with the Code of Conduct's protection for
good-faith reporting.

## Outcome

Once a decision is reached at any level, HR documents it in writing. The
employee may request one review of a Level 3 decision within 10 business
days; beyond that, the decision is final.
