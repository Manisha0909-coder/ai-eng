# Onboarding Policy

**Version:** 2.1
**Effective Date:** 2025-09-01

## Probation Period

Every new hire serves a probation period of **6 months** from their start
date. Probation is about role fit and performance, and is evaluated
separately from annual-leave eligibility — a new hire's 6-month probation
period and their 6-month wait before annual leave eligibility (see the
Employee Leave Policy) happen to be the same length but are unrelated: one
is about performance, the other is purely time-based.

## Onboarding Checklist (First 30 Days)

| Day | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Laptop issued, accounts provisioned, IT security briefing | IT |
| Day 1 | Code of Conduct and handbook acknowledgment | HR |
| Week 1 | Benefits enrollment opens | HR |
| Week 2 | Manager 1:1 goal-setting | Manager |
| Day 30 | 30-day check-in | Manager + HR |

## Benefits Enrollment Window

New hires must enroll in benefits within **30 days** of their start date.
Missing this window means waiting until the next open enrollment period
(see Benefits Overview for your region), except for qualifying life events.

## Probation Review

At the end of the 6-month probation period, the manager submits a
probation review recommending confirmation, extension (up to 3 additional
months, requires department head approval), or termination. Confirmation
is the default outcome unless a review is filed.

## Equipment and Access

IT provisions a laptop, email, and role-appropriate system access on Day 1,
per the Access Management Policy. Remote-work equipment (monitor, chair
stipend, etc.) is requested separately through the Remote Work Equipment
Policy process and is not part of standard Day 1 provisioning.
