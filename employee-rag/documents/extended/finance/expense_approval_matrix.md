# Expense Approval Matrix

**Version:** 1.4
**Effective Date:** 2025-12-01

## Purpose

This policy defines who approves an employee-submitted expense report,
building on the general Expense & Travel Policy's rules on receipts and
submission deadlines. It is separate from the Procurement Policy's
approval matrix, which covers direct company purchases, not employee
reimbursements.

## Approval Thresholds

| Expense Amount (per report) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under ₹5,000 | Manager |
| ₹5,000 – ₹25,000 | Manager + Finance |
| ₹25,000 – ₹1,00,000 | Department head + Finance |
| Over ₹1,00,000 | CFO |

These thresholds apply per expense report, not per individual line item —
a report with many small items that together exceed ₹5,000 still needs
Manager + Finance approval even if no single item does.

## Late Submission

Per the general Expense & Travel Policy, reports submitted more than 30
days after the trip require manager approval regardless of amount — this
additional approval requirement stacks on top of, rather than replacing,
the amount-based thresholds above.

## Rejected Expenses

A rejected expense line item is returned to the employee with a reason.
The employee may resubmit with additional documentation within **14
days**; after that, the line item is considered withdrawn.

## Audit Sampling

Finance audits a random 10% sample of approved expense reports each
quarter, independent of the amount-based approval already received, to
check for policy compliance.
