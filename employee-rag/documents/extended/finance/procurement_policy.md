# Procurement Policy

**Version:** 1.7
**Effective Date:** 2025-12-01

## Scope

This policy covers purchasing goods and services for business use —
software, hardware, office supplies, vendor contracts — separate from the
Expense & Travel Policy, which covers employee-incurred expenses submitted
for reimbursement. Procurement purchases are made directly by the company,
not reimbursed after the fact.

## Receipt Threshold

A receipt or invoice is required for any procurement purchase over
**₹10,000** — a different, higher threshold than the Expense & Travel
Policy's ₹500 receipt requirement for personal reimbursement, because
procurement purchases are typically larger and invoiced directly.

## Approval Matrix

| Purchase Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under ₹10,000 | Manager |
| ₹10,000 – ₹1,00,000 | Department head |
| ₹1,00,000 – ₹10,00,000 | Finance + department head |
| Over ₹10,00,000 | CFO |

See the Expense Approval Matrix for the equivalent thresholds on
employee-reimbursed expenses, which use different amounts.

## Preferred Vendors

Software and hardware must be purchased through IT-approved vendors where
one exists, consistent with the Software Asset Management Policy's
Approved Software List. Using a non-preferred vendor requires a business
justification.

## Purchase Order Process

1. Submit a purchase requisition through the Procurement portal.
2. Requisition routes to the approver per the matrix above.
3. Finance issues a purchase order within **5 business days** of approval.
4. Goods/services may not be ordered before the purchase order is issued,
   except for pre-approved recurring vendor contracts.
