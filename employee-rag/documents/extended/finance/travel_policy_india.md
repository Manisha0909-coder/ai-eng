# Travel Policy (India)

**Version:** 2.1
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

## Applicability

This policy covers domestic and international travel booked by
India-based employees. It supplements, and does not replace, the general
Expense & Travel Policy's rules on receipts, submission deadlines, and
non-reimbursable items.

## Flight Class

Economy class is standard for domestic flights. Business class requires VP
approval for flights over **6 hours**, matching the general Expense &
Travel Policy's threshold — this policy does not change that rule, only
adds India-specific train/rail guidance below.

## Rail Travel

For routes under **4 hours** by train, rail travel in AC 2-tier or higher
is preferred over flying, where a suitable train exists, for cost and
sustainability reasons. This is guidance, not a hard requirement — flying
is still permitted if the schedule doesn't work.

## Per Diem

Domestic business travel receives a daily allowance of **₹1,500** for
meals and incidentals, identical to the general Expense & Travel Policy's
per diem — this policy does not introduce a separate India-specific
number for per diem, only for hotel caps below.

## Hotel Caps

| City Tier | Nightly Cap |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, etc.) | ₹8,000 |
| Tier 2 | ₹5,000 |
| Tier 3 | ₹3,500 |

## Visa and International Travel

For international business travel, visa fees are fully reimbursable with
receipt, submitted within the same **30-day** window as other expenses
under the general Expense & Travel Policy.
