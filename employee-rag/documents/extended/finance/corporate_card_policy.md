# Corporate Card Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-01

## Eligibility

Employees who travel for business at least **quarterly**, or who manage a
team budget, are eligible for a corporate credit card. Request one through
Finance, with manager approval.

## Credit Limit

Standard corporate cards carry a monthly limit of **₹50,000** (or
**$600** for US-based employees). A higher limit requires department head
approval and is reviewed annually.

## Permitted Use

Corporate cards may only be used for business expenses covered by the
Expense & Travel Policy or pre-approved procurement purchases under the
Procurement Policy's threshold for card-eligible categories. Personal use,
even if reimbursed later, is a policy violation.

## Reconciliation

Cardholders must reconcile all charges in the expense system within the
same **30-day** window as standard expense reports. Unreconciled charges
after 45 days result in card suspension until resolved.

## Lost or Stolen Cards

Report a lost or stolen corporate card to the card issuer and Finance
immediately — Finance cancels the card and issues a replacement within
**3 business days**. This is separate from, and in addition to, reporting
a lost company laptop under the IT Security Policy.

## Card Return

Corporate cards must be returned (or destroyed, with Finance's
confirmation) on the employee's last working day, alongside other company
equipment per the Access Management Policy's deprovisioning process.
