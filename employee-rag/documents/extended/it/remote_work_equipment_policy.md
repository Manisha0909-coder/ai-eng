# Remote Work Equipment Policy

**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-01
**Supersedes:** Remote Work Equipment Policy v1.0 (effective 2023-06-01)

## Scope

This policy covers equipment and connectivity for employees working from
home, per the frequency allowed in the Employee Leave Policy's Work From
Home section. It does not cover conduct expectations while working
remotely (core hours, cameras on) — see the Code of Conduct's Remote Work
Conduct section for that.

## Equipment Stipend

Employees working from home receive a one-time home-office setup stipend
of **₹15,000** (or **$200** for US-based employees), covering a monitor,
chair, or similar equipment. This is an increase from the **₹8,000** /
**$120** stipend under the superseded 2023 policy, reflecting higher
equipment costs.

## Monthly Connectivity Allowance

A monthly internet/connectivity allowance of **₹1,500** (or **$25** for
US-based employees) is paid alongside salary, separate from the daily
business-travel meal allowance in the Travel Policy.

## VPN and Security Requirements

All remote connections to internal systems must use the company VPN, per
the IT Security Policy's VPN Access section. The equipment stipend does not
cover personal internet service — only the allowance above does.

## Equipment Ownership and Return

Equipment purchased with the stipend is owned by the employee, not the
company issuing it as loaned hardware — unlike the company laptop, which
must be returned per the Access Management Policy's deprovisioning
section.
