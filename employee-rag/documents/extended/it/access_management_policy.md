# IT Access Management Policy

**Version:** 2.2
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-01

## Account Provisioning

New employee accounts are provisioned on Day 1 by IT, based on the role
template approved by the hiring manager (see Onboarding Policy). Access
beyond the role template requires a ticket approved by the employee's
manager and, for systems holding customer data, the Data Privacy Officer
(DPO).

## Access Review

IT reviews all system access **quarterly** (every 3 months). Managers must
confirm their team's access is still appropriate within **5 business days**
of the review request; unconfirmed access is suspended automatically.

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA is mandatory on all systems holding company or customer data. This is
in addition to — not a replacement for — the password requirements in the
IT Security Policy (minimum 12 characters, rotated every 180 days).
Employees who lose their MFA device must contact the IT Helpdesk
immediately; a temporary bypass requires manager approval and expires in
**24 hours**.

## Deprovisioning

On an employee's last working day, IT disables all account access by end
of day. Equipment (laptop, access badge) must be returned within **5
business days** of the last working day; unreturned equipment is billed to
the employee at replacement cost.

## Privileged Access

Admin-level access to production systems requires separate approval from
the relevant system owner, is reviewed **monthly** rather than quarterly,
and is logged in the access management system for audit purposes.
