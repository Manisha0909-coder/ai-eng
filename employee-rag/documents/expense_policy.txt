Expense & Travel Policy

Reimbursable Expenses:
Employees may claim reimbursement for pre-approved business travel, client meals, and work-related software subscriptions.
Receipts are required for any expense over 500 rupees.

Submission Deadline:
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
Late submissions require manager approval.

Travel Booking:
Domestic flights and hotels should be booked through the company travel portal.
Economy class is standard for domestic travel; business class requires VP approval for flights over 6 hours.

Per Diem:
Employees on approved business travel receive a daily allowance of 1500 rupees for meals and incidentals.

Non-Reimbursable Items:
Personal entertainment, traffic fines, and alcohol are not reimbursable.
