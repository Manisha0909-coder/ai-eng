IT & Security Policy

Password Requirements:
Employees must use a password manager and enable multi-factor authentication on all company accounts.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and rotated every 180 days.

Device Policy:
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before first use.
Personal devices used for work email must be enrolled in mobile device management (MDM).

VPN Access:
Employees accessing internal systems from outside the office must connect through the company VPN.

Software Installation:
Only software approved by the IT team may be installed on company devices.
Requests for new software should be submitted through the IT helpdesk.

Incident Reporting:
Any suspected security incident, phishing email, or lost device must be reported to security@company.com within 24 hours.
