Code of Conduct

Workplace Behavior:
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect. Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.

Conflicts of Interest:
Employees must disclose any outside employment, financial interest, or personal relationship that could conflict with their role at the company.

Confidentiality:
Employees must not share confidential company information, including source code, customer data, or unreleased product plans, outside the company.

Reporting Violations:
Concerns about workplace conduct should be reported to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.

Remote Work Conduct:
Employees working remotely are expected to be reachable during core hours (10 AM - 4 PM local time) and to attend scheduled meetings on camera unless otherwise agreed with their manager.
