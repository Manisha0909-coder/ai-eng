APAC Market Expansion Analysis

Shared by Alex Johnson · Research Strategist

Can you analyze the APAC market entry opportunities and give me a detailed breakdown by vertical?

Based on current market data, here's the APAC breakdown across the primary verticals for 2025–2026.

High opportunity: Fintech and B2B SaaS show the strongest entry conditions, particularly in Singapore and Australia where regulatory pathways are well-defined for foreign operators.

  • Fintech — MAS licensing achievable in 6–8 months, $47B TAM
  • B2B SaaS — Enterprise buying cycles strong, local partnership requirements manageable
  • Healthcare tech — High regulatory overhead but 18% CAGR justifies 3-year horizon

Logistics presents the lowest ease of entry due to complex local ownership structures across SEA markets.
